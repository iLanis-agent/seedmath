SeedMath

Honest seed math: your two frost dates drive the entire season.

The calendar

Every instruction on a seed packet - start indoors 6-8 weeks before last frost, sow after danger of frost passes - reduces to your last spring frost and first fall frost. SeedMath turns those two dates into indoor start, transplant, direct sow, and first-harvest dates for ten common crops.

The successions

Lettuce, carrots, beans, basil, and kale want repeat sowings. SeedMath spaces them by crop and stops at the last sowing whose harvest still beats your fall frost - a sowing that freezes green is compost, not dinner.

The bed

Give it bed dimensions and it counts plants at real spacing, then divides by the crop's actual germination rate to tell you how many seeds - and how many packets - to buy. Packets quote plant counts you will never reach; this fixes that.
