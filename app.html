Whole garden at a glance

First action date per crop: indoor start when the crop needs a head start, direct sow otherwise. Days-to-maturity runs from transplant for indoor crops, from sowing for direct-sown ones - that is the convention on the packet too.

The honest part

Seed packets quote plant counts you will never reach: they assume every seed sprouts. SeedMath divides by the crop's real germination rate, so the packet count covers the stand you actually want. Succession sowings stop at the last date whose harvest still beats your fall frost - a sowing that freezes green is not a sowing, it is compost.
